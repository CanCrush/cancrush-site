The fan experience — about 20 seconds
  1. Stomp an empty can flat on the mat's stomp pad.
  2. Set it on the X and scan the QR — the contest opens in the phone's browser, no download.
  3. Snap one photo. Computer vision measures the crush to a hundredth of an inch.
  4. They're on tonight's leaderboard instantly — enter as many cans as they like (carry the whole row's!).
  5. Round winners get a redemption code on their phone, good at concessions before close.
The arena mat

A leaderboard fans can trust
Measurements are camera-corrected against printed reference marks and calibrated to each installed mat. Every can is visually fingerprinted: re-photographing the same can is caught with a friendly nudge, and the same can can never win twice — even handed to a friend. Boards are split by can size, with one spot and one prize per person per round.
For sustainability sponsors
Every entry is a can physically crushed right at the bin — and the contest counts them, per event, all season. Per event we can report entries, unique entrants, and the live crowd total — numbers a recycling program can publish. Fans leave with one more association: your brand, and the most fun they've had recycling.
Simple on your side
You provide floor space beside the bins, concession redemption of short winner codes, and any screen for the live board. We provide the contest and the morning-after results email to every entrant. Prizes are yours to choose — we recommend concession credits for the stand traffic, but the system just mints short codes; prize wording and value are configurable per event.
Mats, printing, placement — flexible
The mat is designed for certified anti-slip floor-graphic materials and prints from our one-page spec at your or your sponsor's preferred large-format vendor. Every installed mat is scale-calibrated in place, so who prints never affects measurement accuracy. Your crew chooses placement and lays the mats — your floor, your rules.
